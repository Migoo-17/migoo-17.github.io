The short version

  • Your prayer record — which prayers you marked, when, and any reason — is stored privately to your account and synced only to your devices.
  • No one, including a circle-mate, can read another person's prayer data. This is enforced in the database itself, not just in the app.
  • Days you mark as excused always count as complete in a circle, so no one can infer a menstrual cycle from your shared week.
  • Sharing with a circle is off until you turn it on, and you pick whether members see day counts only or individual prayers.
  • Your streaks and completion stats are worked out on your device. Salaty also uses privacy-friendly product analytics (PostHog) to see how features are used and fix bugs — never the content of your prayer journal, and never to build an ad profile.
  • Your approximate location (or a city you choose) is used to calculate prayer times and the Qibla, and is sent to a prayer-time service to build your timetable.
  • Salaty shows ads through Google AdMob. Personalized ads run only with your consent.

2.1Account

You can use prayer times and the Qibla without an account. To keep a prayer journal, sync across devices, or join a circle, you create an account with:

  • Email address and password — handled by our authentication provider (Supabase). Your password is stored only as a salted hash; we never see it.
  • Sign in with Apple or Google — if you choose this instead, the provider gives us a unique identifier, your email address (Apple lets you hide it behind a private relay), and, when available, your display name and profile picture. Apple sends your name only on the first sign-in.

We also store a small profile record: your display name and avatar, the time zone of your prayer-times location, whether you've turned on per-prayer sharing, and whether you've finished onboarding.

2.2Prayer activity & excused periods

When you mark a prayer, Salaty records which of the five daily prayers it was, the calendar date it belongs to, whether it was on time, late, or missed, and an optional reason you can add for a missed prayer (traveling, work or school, overslept, forgot). The date is worked out on your device from your local prayer schedule — never from our server clock.

You can also mark excused periods — date ranges during which prayer is not required (for example menstruation or postnatal bleeding). Salaty stores the start date and, once it ends, the end date.

This data is treated as sensitive. Prayer logs and excused periods are stored strictly private to your account. No sharing rule anywhere in the system lets another person read them. They are mirrored to your own devices for offline use and synced through your account.

2.3Location

Prayer times and the Qibla depend on where you are. You can either let the app use your device location or pick a city from a built-in list.

  • Salaty requests approximate location on Android and while-in-use location on iOS. It does not request background location.
  • Your coordinates, or the city and country you chose, are sent to the Aladhan API (api.aladhan.com, operated by the Islamic Network) to calculate your monthly prayer timetable.
  • We store the time zone of your prayer-times location on your profile so that circle windows line up correctly across regions. We do not store your precise coordinates on our servers.
  • The Qibla direction is computed on your device from your location and does not leave it.

2.4Device motion sensors

The Qibla compass reads your device's accelerometer and magnetometer to work out which way the device is pointing. This happens entirely on the device in real time. None of it is recorded, transmitted, or stored.

2.5Circles

A circle is a small private group — usually one or two people — for praying alongside each other. Circles are entirely optional.

  • If you create or join one, we store the circle name, its join code, and your membership.
  • Other members can see a summary of your recent prayer week: how many days you completed and your counts of on-time, late, and missed prayers over a window (7 days by default). If you turn on "share which prayers", members also see the status of individual prayers by day.
  • Members can send you nudges (a note of encouragement, a du'a, or a clap) and create shared goals. We store these.
  • Sharing is off by default. There are no leaderboards and no ranking.

Excused days count as complete. In a circle summary, any day inside an excused period is counted as a full day and never shrinks your visible week — so a circle-mate cannot infer a menstrual cycle from a dip in your activity. Your own private statistics handle excused days correctly and separately.

Each person's week is measured in their own time zone, so members in different regions see each other fairly rather than permanently "behind".

2.6Advertising

Salaty shows ads through Google AdMob.

  • In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, Salaty shows a consent request before any personalized advertising. On iOS, Salaty also uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • If you consent, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and device information to show personalized ads and measure them.
  • If you decline or are not asked, ads are non-personalized — based only on context, not on tracking.
  • You can reset or clear your advertising identifier at any time in your device settings, and re-open the consent choice from [Settings location, e.g. Settings › Privacy].

Google processes this data as an independent controller under Google's Privacy Policy and its AdMob data-use terms.

2.7Product analytics

To understand which features are used and to find and fix bugs, Salaty uses PostHog, a product-analytics service. It records events such as screens opened, actions taken, and errors encountered, together with your app version, device model, operating system, coarse region, and a random analytics identifier for your installation.

  • Salaty does not send the content of your prayer journal — your prayer marks, reasons, or excused periods — to PostHog.

  • Where the law requires it we ask your consent before analytics, and you can turn analytics off at any time from [Settings location].

PostHog processes this data as our processor under PostHog's privacy policy.

2.8Push notifications

Prayer-time alerts and reminders are scheduled on your device and need no server. Separately, Salaty sends push notifications for [what triggers a push — e.g. circle nudges, goal updates, a circle-mate joining].

  • To deliver them, your device registers a push token with [push provider — Apple Push Notification service / Firebase Cloud Messaging / OneSignal] and with our backend. The token identifies your device installation, not you personally.
  • You can turn push notifications off in your device settings or in the app. Prayer-time reminders keep working either way, because they are local.

2.9App updates

Salaty uses Shorebird to deliver small over-the-air code fixes between store releases. When the app checks for an update it sends a non-identifying app identifier, your platform, and the installed version so Shorebird can return the right patch. It does not send any personal data.

2.10Fonts & network requests

Arabic text is displayed with a typeface loaded at runtime from Google Fonts. As with any network request, Google receives your device's IP address when the font is fetched. Every request the app makes — to our backend, the prayer-time API, AdMob, PostHog, the push provider, and Google Fonts — necessarily discloses your IP address to that service and may appear in its transient server logs.

3How Salaty uses information

We use the information above only to:

  • calculate and display prayer times and the Qibla direction;
  • keep your prayer journal and sync it across your devices;
  • compute your streaks and completion statistics (on your device);
  • run circles you join — membership, shared summaries, nudges, and goals;
  • schedule prayer-time alerts and reminders locally on your device;
  • send push notifications for circle activity you have turned on;
  • authenticate you and keep your account secure;
  • show ads that fund the app, and request consent where required;
  • understand how features are used and diagnose crashes, through product analytics;
  • deliver app updates and fix problems;
  • comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your prayer data for advertising, profiling, or any form of automated decision-making, and we never sell it.

5What Salaty does not do

  • Your prayer journal — marks, reasons, and excused periods — is never sent to analytics or any other third-party service.
  • No advertising profile is built by Salaty from your prayer data or activity.
  • Salaty's servers store no aggregated statistics of your prayer record; streaks and completion are computed on your device.
  • No sale of personal information, and no "sharing" as defined by California law.
  • No mechanism, anywhere, for one user to read another user's prayer logs or excused periods.

To exercise any of these rights, email magdy.temamy@yahoo.com. We will respond within the time your law requires (generally one month). We may need to verify your identity against your account.

8Security

  • All traffic between the app and our services is encrypted with HTTPS / TLS.
  • Every table in our database is protected by row-level access control: your rows are readable only by you. The few cross-user reads that circles need go through vetted server-side functions that enforce the sharing rules themselves.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes by our authentication provider.
  • Circle join codes are drawn from a large random space to resist guessing.

No system is perfectly secure, but we design Salaty so that the most sensitive data is the hardest to reach. If we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.
