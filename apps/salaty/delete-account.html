Salaty · Account & Data Deletion

Delete your account.

You can request deletion of your Salaty account and its data at any time.

Privacy Policy ← Salaty

How to request deletion

To delete your Salaty account, email us from the address associated with your account:

magdy.temamy@yahoo.com  — subject line: "Delete my Salaty account"

We verify the request against your account and complete deletion within 30 days. You'll get a confirmation email once it's done. An in-app "Delete account" option (Settings › Account) will also be available once it ships; this page will be updated to reflect that.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, email, and profile (display name, avatar).
  • Your prayer journal — every prayer mark, status, and reason.
  • Your excused periods.
  • Your circle memberships and any circles only you belong to; shared summaries stop being visible to other members immediately.
  • Your push notification token.

Copies in routine encrypted backups are overwritten on the normal backup cycle, within 30 days of deletion.

What isn't deleted

Some records may be kept briefly where the law requires it — for example transient server logs generated by our infrastructure providers, which rotate out automatically and are not linked back to you once your account is removed.

Partial deletion

You don't need to delete your whole account to remove specific data. From inside the app you can already un-mark or delete individual prayer entries, remove excused periods, and leave a circle — each of these takes effect immediately without contacting us.

Questions

See the full Privacy Policy for more detail on what we collect and why, or email magdy.temamy@yahoo.com with any question about your data.
