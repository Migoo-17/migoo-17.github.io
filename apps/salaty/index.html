Prayer companion · iOS & Android

Salaty.

Private by default. You choose what to share.

Salaty helps you keep up with the five daily prayers: accurate prayer times and Qibla direction wherever you are, a private prayer journal to track your own consistency, and small "circles" for praying alongside a partner, friend, or family member — without ever exposing your prayer data to anyone else.

Privacy Policy ← All apps

What Salaty does

✦Prayer times & Qibla

Accurate prayer times calculated for your location (or a city you choose), plus an on-device Qibla compass — no location data ever needs to leave your phone for direction finding.

✦Private prayer journal

Mark each prayer as on time, late, or missed, with an optional reason. Your journal is strictly private to your account — enforced at the database level, not just in the app.

✦Excused periods, handled fairly

Mark excused periods (like menstruation or postnatal bleeding) and they're always counted as complete in any shared view, so nothing about your cycle is ever inferable by others.

✦Circles

Small, optional, private groups for praying alongside people you trust. Sharing is off by default, and you choose whether members see day counts only or individual prayers.

✦Nudges & goals

Send a circle-mate a note of encouragement or a du'a, and set shared goals together — no leaderboards, no ranking.

✦Built to respect your data

Streaks and stats are computed on your device. Product analytics help fix bugs, never to profile you or your prayer activity — see the full breakdown in the privacy policy.
