About

I'm passionate about mobile technology and its potential to transform industries and elevate user experiences. As the Mobile Engineering Manager at Beyond Menu, I lead a talented team developing and scaling cross-platform mobile apps for the food-delivery and restaurant industry — working closely with product, design, and backend teams to keep our apps fast, reliable, and genuinely useful.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Business Informatics from The German University in Cairo, where I built a foundation in programming, web development, database management, and data analysis. That mix of technical and business grounding is how I approach mobile development — strategically, with an eye on the value a feature actually creates.

Outside of my day job, I build and ship my own apps, and occasionally write about mobile engineering on Medium.
